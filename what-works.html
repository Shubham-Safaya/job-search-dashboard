All figures are heuristic ranges synthesized from public industry surveys (recruiting-platform benchmark reports, published recruiter AMAs, large public salary/offer datasets). They are directional, not guarantees, and they vary hugely by seniority, market, and year. Anyone selling you precision here is selling you something else too.
